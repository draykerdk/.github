# Code of conduct

Drayker's public spaces exist so people can examine ideas, evidence and implementations together.

## Expected

- Be precise, patient and open to correction.
- Critique claims and work, not a person's identity or worth.
- State uncertainty and conflicts of interest.
- Credit sources and contributors.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Keep financial, medical, identity and security discussions within their documented evidence and limits.

## Unacceptable

- Harassment, threats, discrimination or sexualized conduct.
- Doxxing, impersonation or deliberate exposure of credentials.
- Spam, manipulation, fabricated evidence or misleading claims of affiliation.
- Pressure based on promised payment, investment return, tokens, access or authority.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or the integrity of the work. During the founding phase, final enforcement responsibility rests with the steward identified in [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md).

For a sensitive report, request a private channel without including the sensitive details publicly.
